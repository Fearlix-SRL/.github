## 👋 About us

We are a young startup born from the dream of **three friends** who share twenty years of friendship and the same passion for technology and innovation.
In 2023 the **Start&Innova Giovani** programme of the Marche Region allowed us to turn that idea into a company.
Today we design and build custom software for businesses and professionals.

## 🛠️ What we do

| | Service | |
|:-:|---|---|
| 🌐 | **Custom web applications** | Web apps built around your business processes |

| 🤖 | **AI solutions** | Automation, data analysis and decision support |
